About SoilGPT

Soil health intelligence for Indian agriculture

SoilGPT fetches soil health data from authoritative sources like the Government of India's Soil Health Card portal, ICAR-NBSS&LUP, ISRIC's SoilGrids, and WoSIS. It interprets chemical (pH, N, P, K, OC), physical (humidity, texture), and biological parameters to give actionable crop and fertilizer recommendations.
